## Current shape assessment

The front reads as one broad enclosing convex breast, without separate anatomical pectoral cups. Its upper volume passes smoothly into a projecting lower breast and returns inward to a restrained waist. The front and quarter views show a short, nearly level integral flange rather than a pendant point or an invented separate fauld. This is coherent with the rounded reference's compact torso and soft chest-to-belly volume, adapted to the broader supplied wearer.

The side view and current body sections show the designed breast projection forward of the torso, with the back following the shoulder-blade and lower-back envelope more closely. The waist sits at the body narrowing; it does not extend as a rigid skirt over the lower abdomen. The visible upper arm openings leave the shoulders free, and the back's broad neck opening stays below the neck rather than crowding it. The upper front remains visibly forward of the chest, but its curvature and the enclosing section explain that stand-off as part of the breast volume. I do not see a new consequential seating mismatch in these views.

The rear plate now flows broadly around the shoulder blades and into the waist. In the no-shadow rear-quarter image, the near lower armhole turns into the back through a broad smooth transition. The short sharp inward trough that interrupted the plate face in candidates 29 and 33 is absent. A gradual tonal sweep remains, consistent with the plate turning around the flank; it does not read as a localized pinched crease. The ordinary rear image agrees at both armhole corners. This resolves the specific previously blocking appearance rather than merely reducing its darkness.

The front/back edges remain distinct. The quarter and rear-quarter views show an open upper flank transition associated with the arm opening, while the lower flank and waist edges approach one another. The section image supports close lower-flank/back seating and a consistent waist envelope. Small visible edge offsets and the waist seam read at construction scale rather than as a broad gap between unrelated shells. Some of the side junction remains hidden behind the arm, so this is not a numerical overlap certification.

## Consequential mismatches and reservations

No consequential visual mismatch is established by the supplied candidate-42 rounded evidence. The prior rear-corner surface-flow failure is resolved.

The mounted historical references obscure some upper boundaries and do not supply an isolated rear plate matching this wearer, so this PASS is for coherent reference-informed shape within the simple-shell scope, not exact reconstruction of every boundary. The review excludes straps, decoration, textures, separate gussets/plackart/fauld, and material matching. It does not independently accept the other presets' final exports or new ridge-profile changes.

The section picture is useful anatomical evidence, not proof of continuous clearance or valid topology. This review makes no claim about numerical thickness, winding, intersection, all-body support, parameter independence/ranges, animation, or installed runtime behavior.
