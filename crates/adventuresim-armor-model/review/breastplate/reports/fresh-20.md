The rounded shell forms one broad convex enclosure, without anatomical pectoral cups, and returns toward a short waist flange. Tapul introduces a continuous medial ridge and shallow central lower-edge drop. Peascod lowers that central termination and carries its volume farther forward. Fluted retains the rounded carrier with a bounded channel band, smooth upper chest and waist margins, and modest upward fanning. Those distinctions are visibly coherent. The waist sits above the wearer's hip expansion, the neck opening clears the neck, and the rear shell follows the broad back without copying individual muscles. These observations support the major shape direction, but do not resolve the following acceptance failures.

1. **Front/back side relationships remain visibly disconnected in every style.** Front and quarter views show the backplate's side ends standing outside the front shell as separate narrow wings, ending in their own outward corners at the waist. Side views show interrupted front/back flange relationships. The rounded waist and flank sections corroborate separate, offset endpoints rather than a seated lap; this is not inferred from a highlight. The resulting silhouette reads as two independently hanging shells rather than a fitted cuirass assembly. Omitting straps does not itself establish the intended relationship between these plate edges.

2. **The upper arm boundaries have conspicuous angular scallops rather than the references' sustained curved openings.** All four front and quarter views show a projecting upper lobe, abrupt inward notch, and immediate outward return on each front arm edge; side views confirm the pointed inner corner. The same region of Cleveland 1916.1647 and Worcester 2014.736 describes a long, continuous arm cutout. The bare wearer shows no anatomical corner requiring this interruption. This affects the defining outline and how the shell seats beside the shoulder, beyond omitted edging or decoration.

Limits: the supplied references do not establish exact rear construction or quantitative fit.
