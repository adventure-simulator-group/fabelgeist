## Neutral exported GLB

The front retains the broad upper enclosing volume and inward jaw-to-neck contraction. Side retains the projecting nose/visor region, long retreating lower face, and throat return without an outward chin shelf. Quarter retains the small upper gap, continuous rising side seam, and shallow concave lower-visor return seen in candidate 20. No new wide lower skirt, gross visor standoff, visible overlap, seam teeth, or missing component is apparent. The eye and breathing openings occupy the same visible bands. Lighting/resolution differ from the enlarged diffuse baseline, so this establishes visual preservation at the rendered scale rather than pixel identity or numerical equivalence.

## Style samples

- **Wide slots: coherent.** The fewer, wider breathing openings remain separated by substantial visible plate and sit within the projecting visor band. Front is balanced and side/quarter show contained openings with visible cut walls. The main visor boundary, eye slit, and enclosing proportions remain undisturbed. No opening visibly reaches a plate seam or joins its neighbor.

- **Two rows: coherent, with a surface-following shape reservation.** Front clearly reads as two ordered rows of small apertures. Side and quarter show them following the visor's curved/ridged surface; several forward upper-row holes consequently read as bent or chevron-like, while lower-row holes tilt along the lower surface. This is visible and should not be described as uniformly straight rectangular slots in every view. I do not see tearing, a corner crease spreading into the plate, joined holes, or a distorted major seam. Their small size limits detailed corner assessment, and appearance alone cannot certify the remaining material thickness between rows.

- **Asymmetric: coherent at layout scale.** Front shows one continuous eye slit and breathing holes on only one flank. The unperforated flank in side and quarter remains smooth, and removing the center eye bridge has not visibly kinked the opening or changed visor seating. The breathing holes on the opposite flank are seen only obliquely in front; one foreshortens almost to a dot. Thus the intended asymmetry is legible, but these views are insufficient for detailed acceptance of those individual hole shapes. An opposite-side or opposite-quarter close view would be required for that narrower claim.

## Limits

These are three specific style samples on the supplied body, plus the neutral export. They provide no continuous guarantee across aperture counts, widths, row spacing, offsets, blends, other bodies, or combinations not shown. Fine wall/corner details in these 600 by 700 images are less resolved than candidate 20's prior default-only diffuse evidence. No inference is made about hidden intersections, manifoldness, thickness, collision, eye visibility in motion, articulation, runtime installation, or all-body support. The neutral GLB provenance is supplied rather than independently parsed. This report passes the requested visual preservation and sampled coherence, not every potential style or numerical acceptance requirement.
