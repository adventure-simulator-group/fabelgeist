# Pictorial trade signs

The generated hammer, bread peel, and shuttle use tools depicted in the
Nuremberg Mendel Housebook around 1425. Their use on these particular boards,
layout, paint, and establishment association are reconstruction. They do not
claim a documented local guild emblem, privilege, or proprietor.

- [Baker with peel and pointed loaves, folio 35b recto](https://online-service.nuernberg.de/viewer/image/5d64f831-7a9d-47b4-9a01-d6a28f29ad99/74/).
- [Locksmith working with a hammer, folio 39 verso](https://online-service.nuernberg.de/viewer/image/5d64f831-7a9d-47b4-9a01-d6a28f29ad99/83/).
  Assignment to a general smith's trade is an occupational inference.
- [Weaver with hand shuttle, folio 4 verso](https://online-service.nuernberg.de/viewer/image/5d64f831-7a9d-47b4-9a01-d6a28f29ad99/11/LOG_0011/).

Pictograms are authored normalized silhouettes. Text remains the game's
localized establishment label; it is not transcribed sixteenth-century signage.
