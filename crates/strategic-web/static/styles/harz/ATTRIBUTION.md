# Harz construction artwork

The following assets are original geometric vector artwork authored for
Fabelgeist by its contributors:

- `map.svg`
- `skyline.svg`
- `market.svg`
- `inn.svg`
- `church.svg`
- `armoury.svg`
- `frieze-vine.svg`
- `frieze-weave.svg`
- `tracery.svg`
- `ironwork.svg`

Their paths, shapes and patterns are authored construction details. No
external artwork was copied, traced or incorporated, and no generative image
model was used. The accompanying CSS authors the material surfaces, joints,
mouldings and depth. These project-native contributions use the repository's
[AGPLv3 licence](../../../../../LICENSE).

The [construction guide](../../../ARCHITECTURE.md) records the historical
references and their limits. Those sources inform proportions, construction
and the placement of ornament; they are not sources for copied illustrations.
The artwork interprets local buildings and furnishings for the interface. It
does not reproduce the exact appearance of a named building in 1544, and its
colours and individual motifs are design choices.

## External SVG search

Architectural SVGs were searched before these details were authored. The
following reusable geometry was considered but is not included:

- [Architectural trefoil][trefoil], by AnonMoos, released by its creator into
  the public domain.
- [Architectural square quatrefoil][quatrefoil], by AnonMoos with the initial
  vector conversion by Flanker, released into the public domain.
- Game Icons' [wooden door][door], [vine flower][vine] and
  [portcullis][portcullis], by Lorc under CC BY 3.0.

These are generic geometric or semantic designs, not documented Goslar
architectural details. Original SVG geometry fits the component dimensions
and shared relief treatment more closely. Existing semantic Game Icons used
elsewhere retain the [collection's attribution](../../icons/game/ATTRIBUTION.md)
and the repository's [third-party notices](../../../../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Earlier strategic artwork

The PNGs under `../timber-framed/` are separate assets. Their available
provenance must not be conflated with the original Harz vectors:

- The nine public-square, residence and keep masks have explicit
  [project-native provenance](../timber-framed/ATTRIBUTION.md): deterministic
  geometric construction, introduced in commit `f0c0ac05f`.
- The service facades and nine settlement horizons entered the repository in
  commit `580aea157`, [pull request #130][pr130]. The service facades were
  redrawn in commit `a656e64bc`, [pull request #143][pr143]. The latter
  describes authored redraws, but available records do not identify source art,
  licensing method or generation details. Repository history is the verified
  claim; no broader provenance is asserted here.
- Wilderness horizons and props entered in commit `26379cc73`,
  [pull request #142][pr142]. Their art origin is similarly undocumented in
  the reviewed records, and they are outside the Goslar construction assets.

The Goslar pilot replaces the unverified horizon and service masks with the
original Harz vectors or the documented project-native civic and domestic
masks. Older artwork remains outside that pilot.

These notes do not change the licensing or attribution of any older asset.
They identify the boundary of the evidence available for reuse.
