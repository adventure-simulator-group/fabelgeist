Single eagle artwork adapted from Arms of the King of the Romans (c.1433-1486)
by Tom Lemmens (Tom-L) and Heralder (2014), Wikimedia Commons.
Historical model: Wernigeroder (Schaffhausensches) Wappenbuch, southern Germany,
c. 1475-1500, Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Cod.icon. 308 n, scan 23.
A modern single-headed adaptation in the style of the Wernigerode double eagle;
not an independently traced single-headed period specimen.
The dates in the SVG title describe the arms, not the drawing's date.

Modifications by Fabelgeist contributors: shield removal, identified tongue
paths, recoloring, separated painted tones, line widths, asymmetry, reflection,
composition and physical material rendering. The painted marks and simulated
materials are interpretations, not measured manuscript pigments or evidence of
how a corresponding physical shield was decorated.
