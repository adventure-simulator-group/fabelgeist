Double eagle artwork adapted from Arms of the Holy Roman Emperor (c.1433-c.1450)
by Tom Lemmens (Tom-L) and Heralder (2013), Wikimedia Commons.
Historical model: Wernigeroder (Schaffhausensches) Wappenbuch, southern Germany,
c. 1475-1500, Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Cod.icon. 308 n, scan 23.
Traced from the Wernigerode armorial, symmetrized and reproportioned. The modern
drawing replaces the manuscript's small head crowns with red halos; our
adaptation retains those halos.
The dates in the SVG title describe the arms, not the drawing's date.

Modifications by Fabelgeist contributors: shield removal, identified tongue
paths, expanded mirror instance, recoloring, separated painted tones, line
widths, asymmetry, reflection, composition and physical material rendering. The
painted marks and simulated materials are interpretations, not measured
manuscript pigments or evidence of how a corresponding physical shield was
decorated.
