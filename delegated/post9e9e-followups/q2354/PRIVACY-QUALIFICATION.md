# Privacy and replay qualification

The exact private packet remains separately held and unchanged. This successor
redacts matched local filesystem paths and historical audit-root paths. It
retains whole-file identities and the exact private manifest, but removed
bytes cannot be recovered from the successor alone. The public verifier checks
crosswalk structure and file identities; exact byte replay requires a
separately held private original. Five external anchors are required to rerun
the original evidence-only verifier.

Pattern and derived-value scans cover only their stated representations and
bounds. Unknown encodings, undiscovered secrets, Git history, and external
private anchors are excluded. Whole-file hashes do not establish independent
custody.
