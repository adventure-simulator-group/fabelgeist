# Privacy and replay qualification

The exact private packet remains separately held and unchanged. This successor
redacts matched local filesystem paths and historical audit-root paths. Each
whole-file identity and the unmodified private manifest are preserved, but
redacted source bytes cannot be recovered from this successor alone. The
public verifier checks crosswalk structure and current file identities; only a
separately held exact original can demonstrate byte-for-byte replay. Five
external anchors are needed to rerun the original evidence-only verifier.

Pattern and derived-value scans are bounded to described representations;
they do not clear unknown encodings, undiscovered secrets, Git history or the
external private anchors. Hashes of entire files are not proof of independent
custody. The first private prescan did not complete during its initial
observation, and its replacement used a length-bounded email pattern.
