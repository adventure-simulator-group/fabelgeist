# Privacy and replay limits

The exact private original and five anchors remain separately held. This
successor redacts matched local and audit paths plus two non-path agent
identifier occurrences and one code-token candidate. A separate attribution
supplement preserves the reviewer name and relationship. Whole-file identities
and the exact private manifest are preserved, but removed bytes cannot be
recovered from the public successor alone.

Scans cover only stated patterns, representations and bounds; unknown
encodings, undiscovered secrets, Git history and external private anchors are
excluded. Whole-file hashes do not prove independent custody.
