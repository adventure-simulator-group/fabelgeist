   1 # Declared ZIP decoded lengths
   2 
   3 `DeclaredUncompressedByteLength` describes the archive's declared decoded size,
   4 including an NPY header for NumPy array members. It retains zero and the
   5 complete
   6 `u64` width. It does not certify that a member exists, fits an allocation budget
   7 or decodes to that length.
   8 
   9 `ZipArchive::uncompressed_size` returns
  10 `Option<DeclaredUncompressedByteLength>`. NPZ selects an optional member with
  11 `Npz::get`; that handle's `uncompressed_size` returns the declared length
  12 without repeating lookup. NPZ queries retain optional `.npy` suffix matching
  13 and first-member selection. A stored or deflated member may decode successfully
  14 even when the declaration disagrees.
  15 
  16 ```rust
  17 use fabelgeist_numpy_storage::{DeclaredUncompressedByteLength, Npz, NpzArrayName};
  18 
  19 fn declared_size(archive: &Npz) -> Option<DeclaredUncompressedByteLength> {
  20     archive.get(&NpzArrayName::new("weights"))
  21         .map(|member| member.uncompressed_size())
  22 }
  23 ```
  24 
  25 At an external byte-count boundary, `length.bytes()` projects the full declared
  26 quantity into `u64`. Literal and decoding boundaries admit quantities through
  27 `from_bytes`. Display and Debug delegate to the native integer, including its
  28 formatter options. No serialization schema is introduced.
  29 
  30 Rawzip owns compressed ranges and archive-origin positions through private
  31 entry handles. The application no longer defines `CompressedByteLength` or
  32 `ArchiveByteOffset`: those parser-only APIs have no application consumer.
  33 ZIP64 per-entry fields are interpreted by rawzip before the decoded declaration
  34 is admitted. Partial extra fields may retain unreplaced saturation values;
  35 these declarations still do not measure actual bytes.
  36 
  37 Rawzip checks local framing, range overflow and complete compressed payload
  38 before handing back a borrowed slice. An out-of-archive local offset returns
  39 an incomplete-member error rather than overflowing native arithmetic. Stored
  40 members borrow archive bytes; deflated members return owned bytes. Deflate's
  41 initial reservation still casts the decoded declaration to `usize`; no new
  42 allocation budget, checksum, encryption or decoded-size verification is implied.
  43 NPY payload sizes and tensor dimensions remain separate bespoke quantities.
