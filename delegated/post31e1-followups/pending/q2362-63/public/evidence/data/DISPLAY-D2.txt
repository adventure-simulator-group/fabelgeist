   1 # ZIP reader errors
   2 
   3 `ZipArchive::open`, `ZipArchive::from_bytes` and member decoding return
   4 `zip::Result<T>`, whose error is the bespoke `ZipReadError`. Callers match typed
   5 failure classes without interpreting diagnostic prose:
   6 
   7 ```rust
   8 use fabelgeist_numpy_storage::{ZipArchive, zip::ZipReadError};
   9 
  10 match ZipArchive::from_bytes(Vec::new()) {
  11     Err(ZipReadError::MissingEndRecord) => println!("no ZIP directory"),
  12     Err(error) => eprintln!("{error}"),
  13     Ok(archive) => println!("{} members", archive.names().count()),
  14 }
  15 ```
  16 
  17 `MissingEndRecord` represents rawzip's missing-end classification. Other
  18 constructor failures use `Archive` and retain the concrete `rawzip::Error`.
  19 Selected directory corruption rejects admission instead of exposing a partial
  20 successful archive. Rawzip owns record framing and ZIP64 interpretation.
  21 
  22 Member operations distinguish `MissingMember`, `CorruptMember`,
  23 `MemberPastEnd`, `Inflate` and `UnsupportedCompression`. Names retain
  24 `ArchiveMemberName`; unsupported methods retain `ZipCompressionCode` and its
  25 decimal diagnostic. Rawzip's `Eof` becomes `MemberPastEnd`, covering incomplete
  26 local framing as well as missing compressed bytes. Other member parser failures
  27 become `CorruptMember`. Both retain the upstream error as their source. Rawzip
  28 does not expose enough stage information to distinguish every former short
  29 local-header failure from a truncated payload; no second parser recreates that
  30 old distinction. Structural checks still precede unsupported-method errors.
  31 
  32 Native file failures retain `ZipFileOperation::Open` or `Map`, the original OS
  33 path and the concrete `std::io::Error`. Deflate failures retain their original
  34 I/O cause. A rejected path or member name is diagnostic provenance, not proof
  35 of path safety or successful file identity.
  36 
  37 `Npz` keeps its `anyhow::Result` boundary, and errors can be downcast to
  38 `ZipReadError`. Parser and native causes remain reachable through
  39 `std::error::Error::source`; MHR's corrective-basis and sparse-activation
  40 contexts surround the same chain. Default display prints the owning member or
  41 file context. Alternate display also appends native I/O causes; structural
  42 member causes remain in the source chain. NPZ's alternate `anyhow` display
  43 includes those chained parser causes. Archive framing diagnostics include the
  44 upstream explanation directly.
  45 
  46 The adapter retains lossy names, duplicate selection, application count bounds,
  47 central compression authority and declared-size queries. It does not enable
  48 rawzip's optional CRC/decoded-size verifier or add encryption handling and
  49 resource limits. Checked structural access does not certify arbitrary archives.
