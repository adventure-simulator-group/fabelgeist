   1 # fabelgeist-numpy-storage
   2 
   3 NumPy `.npy` and `.npz` reading for Burn, in pure Rust — no NumPy, no C zlib.
   4 
   5 ```rust
   6 use burn::tensor::Device;
   7 use fabelgeist_numpy_storage::{Npz, NpzArrayName};
   8 
   9 let archive = Npz::open("weights.npz")?;
  10 for name in archive.keys() {
  11     println!("{name}: {:?}", archive.array(&name)?.shape());
  12 }
  13 let weights = archive.array(&NpzArrayName::new("layer0"))?.to_tensor::<2>(&Device::default())?;
  14 ```
  15 
  16 - File-backed archives use read-only mappings; in-memory archives own their
  17   input bytes. Members may be stored or deflated, and ZIP64 is handled.
  18 - `NpzMember::uncompressed_size` returns the archive metadata as a bespoke
  19   `DeclaredUncompressedByteLength`. It does not decode or verify actual length.
  20 - Element types: `f4`, `f8`, `i4`, `i8`, `u1`, `b1`.
  21   Decoded numbers use `NpyFloat32Values` and `NpyIntegerValues`; `NpyByteStates`
  22   classifies every payload byte as zero or nonzero. Tensor upload uses
  23   `to_tensor` / `to_int_tensor`, so a `.npz` written as `float64` still loads
  24   into an f32 tensor.
  25 - Fortran-ordered arrays are rejected rather than silently transposed.
  26 
  27 Used by `fabelgeist-mhr` for the MHR pose-corrective tensors.
  28 
  29 ## ZIP structure and byte quantities
  30 
  31 [`rawzip` 0.5.2](https://docs.rs/rawzip/0.5.2/rawzip/) owns end records,
  32 ZIP64 metadata, central-directory framing and bounded member access. Our adapter
  33 owns the 66,000-byte end-record search window, declared member-count bound,
  34 lookup names and decompression policy. Parser errors reject admission rather
  35 than publishing a partially parsed directory. The [count contract](member-count.md)
  36 describes the retained application bound and structural-policy differences.
  37 
  38 The public `DeclaredUncompressedByteLength` remains an unverified archive
  39 declaration. Structural positions and compressed ranges stay inside rawzip's
  40 private entry handles; application APIs do not expose those parser details.
  41 See the [size contract](zip-extents.md).
  42 
  43 ## Archive compression
  44 
  45 The reader classifies rawzip's central-directory method into its supported
  46 Stored or Deflated state, retaining any other method as a bespoke
  47 `ZipCompressionCode` for decimal diagnostics. Unsupported methods remain
  48 queryable metadata and fail when member bytes are requested. Local framing and
  49 compressed bounds are checked before the method error. The central directory
  50 selects the method even when the local header declares a different one.
