# Public source-review transports

These archives retain the separately attributed q24, q185, q689 and q3100
author and peer reports, their available public replay receipts, and correction
chains. `ARCHIVE-MAP.json` identifies every member, including nested manifests.
Run `python3 -B VERIFY.py` to check archived member bytes and modes without
extracting them. Historical readers, revisions and original versus
reconstructed records remain distinct; the reviews do not become checkpoint
3821 evidence. Restricted originals, the separately held key, prior ciphertext
and historical Git/source anchors are needed for the strongest replays.
Same-machine custody is not independent backup. Identity checks do not prove
source semantics, product behavior or lens closure.
