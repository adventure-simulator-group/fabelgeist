     1	# Advancing live audit state while preserving historical checks
     2	
     3	A separate live-state namespace is a viable design to investigate. It is not
     4	an established equivalent verifier, an implementation, an approval, or a
     5	claim that a lens or queue item is complete. Preserve a completed published
     6	checkpoint C as the historical input, run its unchanged checker against an
     7	exact C checkout, and verify evolving state and evidence separately. Choose C
     8	after the in-flight checkpoint is published; this inspection is of committed
     9	publication revision a9b8331ae347542b1ea707b1b04eb8092735d1bd, not staged work.

    19	## Why four frozen files are not enough
    20	
    21	- The a9b driver selects 698 tracked root verifier names and 54 native evidence
    22	  verifiers when no extra matching files exist. It discovers root `verify_*.py`
    23	  and `native-evidence/*/verify.py`, and substitutes qualified privacy
    24	  successors. New matching files silently change what that old driver runs.
    25	- Its qualified `verify_delegated_links_13_059_public.py:20–29` scans root
    26	  `DELEGATED-EVIDENCE-CHECKPOINT*.json` and builds a map whose later matching
    27	  keys overwrite earlier values. A new root checkpoint can alter historical
    28	  matching even if the four state files are unchanged. Several other checks
    29	  compare exact `rglob` or `iterdir` file sets in old packet directories.
    30	- `verify_main_advance_evidence.py:141–156` requires the frozen 13 lens states,
    31	  transition-cell statuses and predecessor queue counts, so even justified
    32	  intermediate transition coverage can conflict. Particle, cloth and action
    33	  verifiers bind particular old partial queue rows, role states, assessments,
    34	  old ledger records and zero counters. Data-directed paths reach registries,
    35	  tree inventories, archives, source proofs and additional verifiers.
    36	- At a9b literal searches find 375 root verifiers naming the 95e baseline, 413
    37	  progress, 273 059 reconciliation, and 448 role coverage. At least 249 match
    38	  one zero-lens pattern and 312 one zero-queue pattern; these regex counts can
    39	  miss chained or indirect assertions and must not be treated as exhaustive.
    40	
    41	A9b has 23,314 tracked entries and 3,071,869,772 summed blob bytes across
    42	paths, not a measurement of disk usage or an independent copy. The retained
    43	NUL-delimited Git tree listing identifies paths, modes, objects and sizes; it
    44	is not a full content rehash or a complete dynamic dependency map. Public
    45	recovery and local object availability need explicit verification.

    47	## Concrete candidate design
    48	
    49	1. Identify C, its full tree and qualified historical driver, source records,
    50	   privacy successors and discovery set. Preserve the entire old tree and all
    51	   data-driven dependencies, not merely baseline, progress, reconciliation and
    52	   coverage JSON. Reproduce C in a separate writable scratch checkout, compare
    53	   expected paths, modes and unfiltered blob bytes before and after the old
    54	   evidence-only driver, and record actual inputs, results and optional checks.
    55	   The recovered verifier creates a temporary native Git clone under its own
    56	   root; P17 verification creates a separate temporary object-only repository.
    57	   A read-only mount alone therefore will not replay the unchanged driver.
    58	2. Keep C's baseline, queue reconciliation, role coverage and progress bytes as
    59	   historical observations. Put the new authority and all new evidence under a
    60	   disjoint namespace such as `live/`: a baseline/revision record, per-row
    61	   reconciliation and source/lens coverage, connected repair ledger, append-only
    62	   dated changes or corrections, source receipts and a separate verifier. The
    63	   exact schema is undecided. Never let a new root `verify_*.py`, native verifier,
    64	   historical checkpoint glob, or old packet directory silently join the old
    65	   checker's discovery set. Treat corrections and reopenings as explicit events,
    66	   not as an assumption that every field changes monotonically.
    67	3. Derive live counters from individually identified rows and evidence, rather
    68	   than accepting hand-edited totals. Bind each original 059 source pointer and
    69	   identity to its recorded revision; bind status changes to named evidence,
    70	   outstanding scope and reviewer attribution. Preserve every old ledger
    71	   relationship and its origin, while explicitly recording new, amended,
    72	   reclassified or superseded work. A complete lens requires source inventory,
    73	   role/queue coverage, source delta, cross-lens relationships and unresolved
    74	   questions to be reconciled under an independently reviewed closure rule.
    75	4. Independently compare the whole live tree against C: classify and bind every
    76	   added, changed, removed and mode-changed path, including any landing page or
    77	   privacy reconstruction, and bind every inherited evidence reference to the
    78	   exact C blob or a separately identified qualified successor. Check new live
    79	   assessments, registered displays, source proof chains, manifest members and
    80	   report/ledger links against actual live bytes. Do not accept a scratch overlay
    81	   check as proof that the live target files match it.
    82	5. Give readers a clearly authoritative live landing and retain the old
    83	   landing as a time-bound observation. At a9b `CONTINUATION.md:3–14` calls the
    84	   old files the current authority; leaving that prose unqualified after a real
    85	   closure would mislead. A filename search found six indirect text references.
    86	   The selected prescan verifier only checks its prior receipt, but other
    87	   dynamic/archive uses were not exhausted. Any changed root landing must be a
    88	   bound successor while old checks replay the C version.
    89	6. Carry a fresh, dated main observation in live state without relabeling 95e
    90	   or silently adopting a newer revision. If authority advances, reconcile the
    91	   full source delta and distinguish its new assessment from earlier evidence.
    92	   Publish the historical identity, live manifest, verification outcomes and
    93	   recoverable references at every checkpoint; record same-machine custody and
    94	   optional private/Git checks honestly.

    96	## Privacy and open questions
    97	
    98	At a9b the 15-file path-digest reconstruction proposal remains unapplied;
    99	522 observed corpus matches and 273 additional semantically selected fields
   100	have distinct evidentiary bases. Its public verifier does not prove live
   101	integration, private replay or complete privacy. If the tip is corrected,
   102	retain original and reconstructed identities with a reviewed crosswalk and
   103	replay the old exact C checkout separately. A forward change does not erase old
   104	Git history or prior copies. Freezing all old files in the new tip would also
   105	freeze those known values, so privacy correction and immutable history need
   106	separate treatment; neither can be silently traded away.
   107	
   108	An independently reviewed live schema and closure rule, a complete dynamic
   109	input and discovery audit, byte-exact historical replay, live-target binding,
   110	privacy review and recovery verification remain open. This packet implements
   111	none of them. It does not establish whole-goal completion, product behavior or
   112	independent-machine custody.
