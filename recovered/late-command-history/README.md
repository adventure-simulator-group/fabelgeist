# Late checkpoint command history

These 359 historical command-execution records cover transcript ordinals
417000 through 419530, including 17 recorded failures. They preserve command
bodies, captured outputs, recorded status, exit codes and known truncation
indicators. The runtime wrapper and Python startup warning were removed;
local paths, URLs and email patterns were redacted. Original and published
hashes are recorded separately. Other output may have been intentionally
sliced or redirected, and this selection omits non-command transcript events.

The records overlap other recovery sets. They do not recreate the final
ledger, queue, receipts or authoritative checkpoint. Historical commands are
inert evidence: do not replay them without independently restoring their
inputs and reviewing their effects.
