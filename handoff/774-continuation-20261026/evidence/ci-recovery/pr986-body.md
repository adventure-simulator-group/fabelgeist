## Implementation context

Shoot identity comes from the producer's deterministic seed and intentionally
changes shoot-salted leaf realization. Software source math preserves the
geometry fingerprint boundary across native hosts; mesh tessellation,
rasterization and shaders retain their separate native arithmetic contracts.
The bounded cloud cases do not claim repeated full-image equality.

The final candidate adds only the two generated outputs after the exporter's
source revision; executable source is unchanged. Independent source and
artifact reviews verified those contents. The existing full-width integer
hash and source-size ceilings are preserved, with the two justified skeleton
reductions described above.
